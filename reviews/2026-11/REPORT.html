Found during validation and fixed

  • Tooling cold: a corrected tar sent back for re-fetching was compared against the wrong size and downloaded forever (high).
  • Hauler: a wait for a server out of rotation blocked the venue's whole queue; idle workers did not wake for small files (medium).
  • Hoarder: a late channel-less refusal from OKX or Kraken dropped a valid channel (medium).
  • Tooling cold: updating-sides ledger lines counted as stocked, so evict could act on a month still being written; --dry-run was silently ignored on push.
  • Prospector: exclusion rows for Bybit's two servers overwrote each other.
  • CI: timing-sensitive suites failed under parallel load; the recursive test run now goes one package at a time.
